Features

Everything you expect from a YouTube client, with a modern interface on top.

🎵 Mini player

Keep watching while you browse — the video shrinks to a mini player at the bottom of the screen.

🔊 Background playback

Audio keeps playing when you leave the app, with play, pause and stop controls in the notification.

▶️ Autoplay

Videos keep playing one after another, with the toggle right inside the player.

👆 Double-tap to seek

Double-tap left or right to jump back or forward 5 seconds, YouTube-style.

🐛 Crash reporter

If the app crashes, the crash log is shown on the next launch — copy it or report it on GitHub in one tap.

🚫 No ads, no tracking

Watch without ads and without a Google account. Your data stays on your device.

⬇️ Downloads

Download videos to watch offline, and pick the resolution that fits your connection.

🔖 Bookmarks & playlists

Save videos for later, build playlists, and import your YouTube subscriptions.

⏩ SponsorBlock

Skip sponsored segments automatically, and adjust playback speed to your liking.

📱 Made for old devices

Lightweight and built for legacy Android — it keeps older phones useful as media players.

Troubleshooting & FAQ

Installation is blocked — what do I do?

Enable “Install from unknown sources” in your Android settings, then open the APK again.

The app crashed. How do I report it?

Just reopen the app — a dialog will show the crash log. Tap “Copy log” or “Report on GitHub” and paste it into a new issue. The log contains only the app version, Android version, device model and the error itself — no personal data.

The Featured tab is empty.

The Featured feed needs a YouTube API key, which is not bundled with the app. Home, Search and Subscriptions work without one.

A video won’t play or shows an error.

YouTube changes its internals from time to time. First, update to the latest HiTube release — stream selection falls back to the best available stream automatically. If it still fails, please report it with the video link.

Is HiTube free?

Yes — free and open-source under the GPL-3.0 license, forever.
